Meet Rebecca

“The whimsically sophisticated” is always a good place to start.

Formally trained in Studio Art and Anthropology at Bucknell University, Rebecca brings a love of culture, folklore, and storytelling to bright oceans, green grasses, yellow suns, red umbrellas, and pink polka dots.

Rebecca began painting on canvas, then added greeting cards, prints, hand-painted steel sculptures, and handmade frames. Her husband Tim handcrafts the distressed wood frames that complement each brightly colored design.

With their three children, Tim and Rebecca continue to show work up and down the East Coast. Since 2009, they have also called River to Sea Gallery home—a permanent gallery space along Wilmington’s Cape Fear River that showcases more than 60 local and regional artists.
