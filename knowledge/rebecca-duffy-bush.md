# Rebecca Duffy Bush Artwork

Rebecca Duffy Bush began painting on canvas and later expanded into greeting cards, prints, hand-painted steel sculptures, and handmade frames. Her husband, Timothy Bush, makes distressed wood frames for the artwork.

Her work includes trains, barnyard animals, octopi, mermaids, and other whimsical subjects. The original site describes her work as “whimsically sophisticated.”

Rebecca and Tim have three children and show their work along the East Coast.

Since 2009, Rebecca has maintained a permanent space at River to Sea Gallery, 224 South Water Street #1B, Wilmington, North Carolina 28401. River to Sea Gallery is along the Cape Fear River in historic downtown Wilmington and represents more than 60 local and regional artists.
